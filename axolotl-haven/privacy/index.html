六角恐龍保育員（Axolotl Haven）隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

本政策說明 iPhone 版「六角恐龍保育員 Axolotl Haven」（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理你的資料。 本遊戲是單機遊戲，開發者沒有架設伺服器，也不建立帳號。

1. 遊戲進度

  • 六角恐龍、泡泡、珍珠、研究等進度只儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者。
  • 刪除本遊戲即會一併刪除這些資料，且無法復原。你可以在遊戲「設定」中匯出存檔碼自行保管。

2. 沒有應用程式內購買

本遊戲沒有任何收費項目。「寶物抽獎」只使用遊玩時賺取的遊戲內貨幣。

3. 廣告（Google AdMob）

玩家可自願觀看獎勵影片換取遊戲內獎勵；不看也可以正常遊玩。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供， Google 可能蒐集以下資料以投放與衡量廣告：

  • 裝置識別碼（僅在你於系統的「允許 App 追蹤」提示中同意時，才包含廣告識別碼 IDFA）
  • IP 位址、大致位置（由 IP 推估）、裝置型號與系統版本
  • 廣告互動紀錄與診斷資料

若你拒絕追蹤，仍可觀看廣告，只是非個人化廣告。你可以隨時在 「設定 > 隱私權與安全性 > 追蹤」中變更。

Google 如何使用資料： https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. 兒童

本遊戲並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，開發者不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

5. 真實的六角恐龍

六角恐龍是瀕危物種。請勿把寵物六角恐龍放生到野外。

6. 政策變更與聯絡方式

政策如有變更，將更新本頁並修改生效日期。如有任何問題，請來信： hochunyin12397@gmail.com

Axolotl Haven Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Axolotl Haven 六角恐龍保育員" ("the Game") handles your data. The Game is single-player. The developer runs no servers and creates no accounts.

1. Game progress

  • Your axolotls, bubbles, pearls, research and other progress are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.
  • Deleting the Game deletes this data permanently. You can export a save code in the game's Settings.

2. No in-app purchases

Nothing in the Game costs money. The treasure draw uses only in-game currency earned by playing.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players may choose to watch rewarded videos for in-game bonuses; the Game is fully playable without them. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the following to deliver and measure ads:

  • Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the system prompt)
  • IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device model and OS version
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

If you decline tracking you can still watch ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Real axolotls

Axolotls are critically endangered. Never release a pet axolotl into the wild.

6. Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: hochunyin12397@gmail.com
